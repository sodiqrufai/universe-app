UniVerse Privacy Policy
Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information UniVerse collects from students, why we collect it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. UniVerse is a campus platform for Nigerian university students. We handle your information in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and applicable regulations.

2. Information we collect
a) Information you give us: your email address, password (stored only in scrambled form, never readable by us), full name, username, profile photo, bio, university, faculty, department and level.
b) Content you create: posts, comments, reactions, stories, marketplace listings, service listings, events and bookings, messages you send, and anonymous posts and comments.
c) Student verification: to confirm you are a student, you may upload a photo of your student ID and give your full name and matriculation number. See section 4.
d) Technical and usage information: device type, app version, approximate activity such as screens used and errors, and your IP address when you use the service. We use this to keep the service secure, fix bugs and understand what to improve.
e) Feedback you send us, including the screen and app version you were using.

3. Why we use your information, and our legal basis
- To create and run your account and provide the service (performance of our agreement with you).
- To verify that users are real students and keep the community safe (our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and abuse).
- To moderate content, handle reports and enforce our Terms (legitimate interest and, where required, legal obligation).
- To send you service messages such as email confirmation, password reset, security notices and notifications about activity you asked for (performance of our agreement).
- To measure reliability and improve the product (legitimate interest). We do not use your content to show you advertising.
- Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting what happened before.

4. Student ID verification
Your ID photo and matriculation number are used only to confirm your student status. The photo is stored in a private storage area that is not publicly accessible; only authorised UniVerse administrators can view it, using short-lived secure links, and approvals and rejections are logged. We do not share your ID with other users, universities or third parties unless the law requires it. We keep a verification record for as long as your account exists so we can show your verified status; you can ask us to delete your ID photo at any time (see section 9), though this may remove your verified badge.

5. The Anonymous community
Posts and comments you make in the Anonymous community are shown to other users only under an anonymous username and never with your name or profile. For safety, we do keep an internal link between an anonymous post and your account. Only a limited group of senior administrators can look up that link, and only after a user has reported content as seriously harmful, and each lookup is recorded in an audit log. Anonymous does not mean untraceable if a post breaks the law or seriously harms someone.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:
- Service providers who help us run UniVerse, under contract and only for our purposes: hosting (Render), database, authentication and file storage (Supabase), email delivery [EMAIL PROVIDER, e.g. Resend or Brevo], error monitoring (Sentry) and [ANALYTICS PROVIDER, if enabled]. Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria. We use them only where they provide appropriate safeguards for your information.
- Other users, to the extent you choose to make something visible (for example your name, username, photo and posts on the Feed, or a listing on the marketplace).
- Authorities or other parties where the law requires it, to enforce our Terms, or to protect people from harm.

7. How long we keep information
We keep your account information while your account is active. When you delete your account from Settings, we delete your login, profile and the content linked to your account straight away, and we remove your profile photo, student-ID photo, post images and story media from storage. We may keep a small number of records where we must meet legal obligations, resolve disputes or prevent abuse (for example moderation records and audit logs, kept up to [12] months). Some content other people already received (such as a message in someone else's chat) and encrypted backups can take up to [30] days to disappear completely. Feedback and public sign-up emails are kept until you ask us to delete them.

8. Security
Passwords are stored in scrambled form. Data is encrypted in transit. Access to personal data is limited to people who need it and administrator actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights and freedoms we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and you as the law requires.

9. Your rights
Under the NDPA you may ask us to: give you a copy of your personal information; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to some uses; and move it to another service where applicable. You can edit most information in your Profile and delete your account from Settings. For anything else, email [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any event within the period the law allows. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

10. Children
UniVerse is for university students who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a younger person has registered, contact us and we will remove the account.

11. Landing page and public forms
If you join a university waitlist or send a partnership enquiry on our website, we store the details you enter (such as your email, university and message) only to respond to you and to decide where to launch next. Ask us to delete them at any time.
