# UniVerse Usability Test: Facilitator Script

**Session length:** about 30 minutes. **Who:** one student per session, one facilitator, ideally one silent observer.
**Goal:** find where first-time users get stuck. We are testing the app, not the student.

## Before the student arrives
- [ ] Run the checklist in `docs/FOUNDER_RUNBOOK.md` section 7 (backup done).
- [ ] Phone charged, on Wi-Fi or data, UniVerse installed or the web app open on its first screen. Signed out.
- [ ] Prepare a fresh email address for the student to use (or let them use their own).
- [ ] Screen recording on (only with the student's OK).
- [ ] Observer notes sheet (`2_observer_notes.md`) printed or open.

## Opening (read aloud)
"Thanks for helping. UniVerse is an app for university students. I'm testing the app, **not you**, so you can't
do anything wrong. Please think out loud: say what you're looking at, what you expect, and anything confusing.
I may not answer questions right away, since I want to see what you'd do on your own. You can stop at any time.
With your permission I'll record the screen and your voice so I can learn from it. It won't be shared outside the team.
Any questions?" 
Get a clear yes/no on recording before starting.

Warm-up: "Which university are you at, and which apps do you use most on campus?"

## The 6 tasks
Hand over the phone. Read one task at a time. Do not explain how. If stuck for over 2 minutes, note it,
then give a small nudge, and note that you did.

1. **Create an account and sign in.** "You've just heard about UniVerse. Download/open it and create your account."
   *Watch:* sign-up steps, username, email confirmation, any error.
2. **Join your university.** "Set up your profile so the app knows your university, department and level."
   *Watch:* finding the university/department (including a missing one), the level screen.
3. **Post something on the campus feed.** "Share a short post with everyone on your campus."
   *Watch:* finding the create button, adding text/photo, seeing the post appear.
4. **Post anonymously and comment.** "Make an anonymous post, then comment on someone else's."
   *Watch:* whether they understand it is anonymous, the username screen, comment display.
5. **Get verified.** "Get your student status verified."
   *Watch:* finding verification, uploading an ID photo, understanding what happens next.
6. **Find help or send feedback.** "You've found a bug. Tell the UniVerse team about it."
   *Watch:* whether they find the feedback/help option in settings or profile.

## 3 closing questions
1. "What was the most confusing or frustrating moment?"
2. "What did you like most, or what would make you use it every day?"
3. "On a scale of 1 to 5, how likely are you to recommend UniVerse to a friend, and why?"

## Closing
Thank them. Share the short feedback form (`3_post_session_feedback_form.md`). Note: do not promise any features or dates.
Immediately after: spend 5 minutes completing the observer sheet while it's fresh.

## Facilitator rules
- Don't lead ("Click the plus button"). Ask: "What would you do next?" or "What are you looking for?"
- Silence is fine. Count to ten before helping.
- Note exact words. Quotes beat summaries.
- If the app crashes or the server is slow (the first load can take about a minute), note it and say so; slow-start is a known issue, not the student's fault.
- No personal data in the notes: use "P1, P2, P3".
