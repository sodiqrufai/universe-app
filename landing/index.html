Questions, answered

Which universities can use UniVerse?

UniVerse is live at Osun State University (UNIOSUN) first. Use the search above to check your school — if it isn't live yet, join the waitlist and we'll email you when it launches.

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. UniVerse runs in your phone's browser and can be added to your home screen so it behaves like an app. See the steps above.

How does verification work?

After signing up you submit proof that you're a student. Our team reviews it, and once approved you get a verified badge.

Is Anonymous really anonymous?

Anonymous posts show a separate anonymous username instead of your name or profile, so other students can't see who you are.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen tap Forgot Password? and we'll email you a link to choose a new one.

I'm a Students' Union or school office. How do we get involved?

We'd love to talk. Use the partnership form below and we'll get back to you.
